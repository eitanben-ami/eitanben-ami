## 🧑‍🔬 About Me

I am a **Research Scientist** focused on the intersection of **Cyber-AI, Fully Homomorphic Encryption (FHE), Multi-Agent Systems (MAS), and Foundation Models**.

I build **evaluation frameworks** and systems that enable **autonomous intelligence** to be measured, audited, and trusted — bridging applied cryptography, machine learning, and secure systems.

- 🗂️ Repositories: [eitanben-ami](https://github.com/eitanben-ami/eitanben-ami)

---

## 🔬 Current Interests

- 🔐 **Privacy-Preserving AI** — FHE, secure inference, and cryptographic guarantees in ML
- 🤖 **Multi-Agent Systems** — coordination, evaluation, and autonomous decision-making
- 🧪 **Evaluation Frameworks** — robustness, trustworthiness, and measurable intelligence
- 🪶 **Foundation Models** — capability assessment, emergent behavior, and benchmarking
